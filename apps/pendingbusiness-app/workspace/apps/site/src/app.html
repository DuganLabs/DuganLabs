Thesis · founder note · roadmap

Thesis

Non-profit, member-funded financial operating system. Route income through yield-bearing vaults until the last responsible moment. Pay bills, taxes, and debt on deterministic schedules that maximize the float between “earned” and “spent.” Surplus returns to members.

Roadmap

  1. Phase 1 — canonical routing (live).
  2. Phase 2 — float capture for quarterly estimated taxes. T-bill staging, yield accrual on the reserve.
  3. Phase 3 — S-Corp transition modeling, Augusta rule, debt extinction projections.
  4. Phase 4 — member onboarding, dues-tier billing, surplus redistribution.
