Float is the product.

Idle money should earn yield every day it isn't needed elsewhere. Bills get paid at the last responsible moment. Debt is extinguished by APR-ordered avalanche, not feel-good snowball. W-4 withholding is tuned to capture safe-harbor-floored tax float in a HYSA.

PendingBusiness is the operating system that does all of that deterministically. It is built for one person and a small group of invited members.

Principles

  1. Float is the product. Every dollar earns yield every day it isn't needed elsewhere.
  2. Pay at the last responsible moment. due_date + grace_period_days, never before.
  3. Avalanche, not snowball. Highest APR first. Snowball costs real money.
  4. Tax math is correct. 2026 IRS brackets, safe harbor rules, FICA caps. No hand-waving.
  5. Passkey-only. No passwords, no Basic auth, no shared secrets.
